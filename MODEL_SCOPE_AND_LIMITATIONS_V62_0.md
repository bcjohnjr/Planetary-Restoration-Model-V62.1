# Model scope and limitations - V62.0

## Evidentiary hierarchy

1. **EXTERNALLY_EXECUTED** — current Run10 FaIR/Hector calculations executed in GitHub Actions with machine-verification gates.
2. **LEGACY_MECHANISTIC** — preserved quantitative calculations from the model lineage that have not been independently calibrated to the standard of the Run10 climate core.
3. **LITERATURE_SCREEN** — numerical or conditional screens anchored to published/official sources.
4. **ENGINEERING_SCENARIO** — mass/energy/design constructs requiring deployment validation.
5. **POLICY_SCENARIO / SERVICE_CAPACITY_SCENARIO** — author-defined sensitivities, not forecasts.
6. **DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY** — retained to inform next experiments but not suitable for a headline predictive claim.

## Important limitations

- The canonical CDR pathway was designed before the current inverse FaIR closure and is not proven globally optimal.
- The inverse control is a constant post-2183 CDR increment. Time-varying controls can produce different annual and cumulative requirements.
- A median 280 ppm criterion does not guarantee 280 ppm for every constrained FaIR configuration; V62 also retains p90 criteria.
- FaIR and Hector provide valid within-model paired counterfactuals, but their future non-CO2 forcing architectures are not identical across models.
- OSCAR has not been executed in the current close-out; do not state that all external validation is complete.
- Surface-ocean pH remains a fixed-temperature/salinity diagnostic screen. Publication-grade threshold timing requires temperature-coupled carbonate chemistry.
- Coral recovery dates are conditional biological windows, not a prediction that the Great Barrier Reef is restored in a particular calendar year. Regional marine heatwave/Degree Heating Week coupling is required for such a forecast.
- Fishery recovery is stock- and management-specific. Cultured fish and seaweed habitat are not counted as wild-stock rebuilding.
- Seaweed engineering, Grid Harvesting and fuel-production calculations require field-scale validation, lifecycle assessment and ecological safeguards.
- Plastic cleanup is tracked as pollution/material recovery and is not automatically credited as CDR.
- Carbonite is a policy/finance hypothesis. Gross settlement revenue envelopes are not equivalent to disposable climate revenue; adoption, incidence, regulation, liquidity, capital formation and political acceptance remain open.
- Proof of Rent (PoR), geotagged tree anchoring and tree-value growth are system-design concepts; the model does not assume tree appreciation automatically beats inflation or automatically creates money supply.
- Robot-doctor and robot-lawyer modules model assisted service capacity. They do not assume autonomous replacement of physicians, licensed lawyers or judges. Clinical/legal validation, human oversight, privacy/confidentiality, liability and jurisdictional compliance are required.
- Universal healthcare, hunger elimination, housing, education and legal access are resource/policy screens and should not be reported as guaranteed outcomes.
- Legacy maritime, tidal, materials, wastewater, data-center and banking numbers are preserved for systems integration but need periodic source refresh before publication as current market estimates.

## Accounting protections

The model keeps separate ledgers for CDR, avoided emissions, energy, materials, ecosystem recovery, pollution cleanup and human services. Benefits cannot be silently moved between ledgers. All removals require additionality, MRV and durability/permanence treatment before being counted as CDR.
